SHREE RADHE GOVIND MUSIC — MULTI-PAGE WEBSITE
Artist: Vinay Kumawat

PAGES
- index.html: Home (13 compact content sections)
- about.html: Artist introduction, credentials, teaching approach, mission
- services.html: Live programs and event services
- classes.html: Singing and music-learning enquiries
- instruments.html: Instrument spotlight and enquiries
- gallery.html: Filterable photos + separate video cards
- areas.html: Nagaur, nearby and outstation service-area enquiries
- reviews.html: Five review texts supplied by the website owner
- contact.html: Separate event booking and music-class WhatsApp forms
- faq.html, privacy.html, terms.html

RUN
1. Extract the ZIP file.
2. Open index.html in a browser, or open the folder in VS Code and use Live Server.
3. Internet is needed for Google Fonts and external illustrative instrument images. The singer photos and logo are local, compressed WebP assets.

CENTRAL CONFIGURATION
Edit config.js to update artist/brand name, phone, WhatsApp, email, Instagram, location and video metadata. Header, footer and floating contact icons are generated from common.js and shared across all pages.

VIDEOS
No video files/links were supplied in the uploaded materials. Gallery includes three separate video cards. Add real YouTube/Instagram URLs in config.js and update the card links before publishing. Do not leave blank links presented as working playback.

CONTACT FORMS
Forms open WhatsApp with a prefilled message; they do not save enquiries to a database. Test both phone and WhatsApp numbers before going live.

NOTES
- Palette follows the supplied Shree Radhe Govind logo: wine/maroon, gold and cream.
- Images supplied by the artist were resized/compressed to WebP for quicker loading.
- Google Fonts are loaded remotely; local fallback fonts are configured.
- Formal degree, awards, teaching employment, prices, timings and outstation availability were not invented. Add only verified details.
- Review texts are from the owner-supplied message; confirm permission to publish names and reviews.
